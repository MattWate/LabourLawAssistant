Data Deletion Instructions

Users of the Justine Labour Law Assistant may request deletion of their personal information at any time.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to support@dubyahinnovation.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the phone number associated with your WhatsApp account.

We will process valid requests within a reasonable period, subject to any legal obligations requiring retention of certain records.

For questions, contact support@dubyahinnovation.com.
