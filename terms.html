2. Justine is an AI assistant, not a person. She is an artificial intelligence system that uses AI models supplied by third parties and works from labour law material approved by VRS. 3. General information, not legal advice. Justine gives general information and a preliminary view of your situation. It is not legal advice and you should not act on it alone. Using Justine does not make you a client of VRS: that relationship starts only when we accept your instructions after reviewing your matter and it covers only the service you have paid for. We may decline to act, for example where we have a conflict of interest. Justine cannot represent you at the CCMA, a bargaining council or a court.

4. Time limits keep running. Using Justine does not stop or extend any legal time limit. An unfair dismissal dispute, for example, must generally be referred to the CCMA or a bargaining council within 30 days of the dismissal, and an unfair labour practice dispute within 90 days. If a deadline is close, refer the dispute yourself or contact us directly without waiting for Justine.

5. Paid letters. Where Justine indicates that a letter may help, you can pay VRS to prepare one. The fee is shown before you pay, and PayFast processes the payment, so we never receive your card details. A VRS consultant reviews your matter and the draft letter, and nothing is sent until that consultant approves it. We aim to complete this review within one business day of payment. If we decline to act or decide not to release a letter, we refund your fee in full. The letter is a without prejudice settlement proposal based only on what you have told us, so please make sure that information is true and complete. It is not a referral of a dispute. Once the letter is approved, you authorise us to send it to the employer contact you gave us, with a copy to you. Because you ask us to start as soon as you pay, the seven-day cooling-off right under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 does not apply once our review has begun.

6. Your personal information. VRS is the responsible party for your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”). We collect what you share with Justine, including your name and contact details, your employer’s details and the facts of your matter. We use it to assess your matter, to prepare and send any letter you pay for, to communicate with you and to keep proper records. Sharing it is voluntary, but Justine cannot assess your matter without it. Please give a personal email address rather than a work address your employer can access, and share details about other people only where your matter needs them.

7. Sensitive information. Some of what you tell Justine may be special personal information, such as details of your health, your trade union membership or an allegation that you committed a criminal offence. We process it only with your consent, which you give through the second consent box or your WhatsApp acceptance, and only for your matter.

8. Who sees your information. Our staff see it, as do the service providers who run Justine for us under confidentiality and security obligations: Anthropic (the AI model), Meta (WhatsApp), PayFast (payments) and our hosting and email providers. Some of them store or process information outside South Africa, including in the United States, and with your consent your information may be transferred to them for these purposes. Our service provider does not use your information to train its AI models. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your permission.

9. Automated assessment. Justine’s score and preliminary view come from automated processing. They do not decide your legal rights or whether we can help you. A VRS consultant reviews every matter before any letter is released, and you may ask us to explain how your matter was assessed and to review that assessment.

10. How long we keep it. We keep your information only for as long as we need it. If you do not go ahead with a paid service, we delete your conversation or remove anything that identifies you within 12 months. If you do, we keep your file for five years after your matter closes, unless the law requires longer. We may use conversations from which you can no longer be identified to improve Justine.

11. Your rights. You may ask for access to your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to its processing or withdraw your consent. To do any of these, email our Information Officer at info@vrslabourlaw.co.za. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that has already happened, though we may then be unable to continue with your matter. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator through www.inforegulator.org.za or by email to POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

12. Fair use. Use Justine only for a workplace matter that concerns you directly or that you are authorised to handle. Do not use her to harass anyone or try to make her act outside her purpose. We may end any conversation that breaches this clause.

13. Limits on our responsibility. Please read this clause carefully. Justine’s general information is given as it is, without any guarantee that it is complete or correct. To the extent the law allows, VRS is not liable for any loss you suffer because you relied on Justine’s general information instead of taking advice or because information you gave us was incomplete or inaccurate. Nor are we liable for the failure of a service outside our control, such as WhatsApp, your internet connection or a payment provider. Where we are liable in connection with a paid letter, our total liability is limited to the fee you paid for it. Nothing in this notice limits our liability for gross negligence or excludes any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 that cannot lawfully be excluded.
